# LUXURY DENTAL CLINIC DESIGN SYSTEM

- Aesthetic: Clinical luxury, spotless hygiene, scientific credibility, radiant confidence.
- Color Tokens:
  - Background Primary: Crisp Medical Slate (`#F8FAFC`) and Pure White (`#FFFFFF`)
  - Secondary / Accents: Deep Clinical Navy (`#0F172A`), Sage Medical Teal (`#0D9488`), Champagne Gold (`#D97706`)
  - Surfaces: Elevated pure white cards with subtle glow borders (`border-slate-100 shadow-sm`)
  - Text Primary: Midnight Slate (`#0F172A`) and Cool Steel (`#475569`). Avoid pure `#000000`.
- Typography:
  - Headers: `Outfit` (modern, geometric, clinical authority).
  - Body: `Plus Jakarta Sans` (neutral, crisp, effortless legibility).
- Interactive Components:
  1. Interactive Before & After Transformation Slider: Visual drag-handle component.
  2. Concern-First Symptom Navigation: Clickable condition pills ("Tooth Pain", "Crooked Teeth", "Missing Tooth", "Yellow Stains").
  3. Doctor Profile Cards: High-res portraits, MDS credentials, IDA council badges.
  4. Toast Notifications: Smooth Sonner alerts for appointment bookings.
